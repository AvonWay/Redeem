What Is “Redemption of Your Estate”?

Core idea as taught by proponents

Supporters of “redemption of your estate” claim that:

  • A separate legal entity (often shown as your NAME IN ALL CAPS) was created using your birth certificate.
  • This entity is sometimes called a “strawman” or “corporate person.”
  • They say this entity is tied into a hidden estate or trust connected to national debt and international banking.
  • “Redeeming” your estate means reclaiming control of that trust and acting as the living man or woman instead of the corporate persona.

How mainstream law views it

In conventional law:

  • A birth certificate is a vital record, not proof of a secret personal bank account or tradable estate.
  • Courts do not treat capitalization of your name as creating a separate legal person; it is still you.
  • Real trusts and estates exist, but they must be created and managed under normal trust and probate rules, not implied from paperwork like a birth certificate alone.

Simple Example

Imagine John Smith is charged with a traffic offense.

Proponents might argue that “JOHN SMITH” is a corporation and the living John is not subject to the court. Mainstream courts, however, treat both as the same legal person and proceed with the case.

Myth vs Reality

Myth: “My birth certificate proves a multi‑million‑dollar account in my name.”

Reality: No official law or government document confirms such accounts; attempts to access them are typically rejected and may be seen as fraudulent.

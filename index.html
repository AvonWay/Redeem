What this site is about

This site explains the core ideas behind “redemption of your estate” and “claiming your birthright,” including how supporters say these ideas connect to banking, trusts, and your legal identity. It also shows where mainstream law and courts agree or disagree with those claims, using real history and documented sources.

What this site is not

  • It is not legal, tax, or financial advice.
  • It does not sell “secret” paperwork, templates, or guaranteed fixes.
  • It does not encourage you to ignore courts, taxes, or regulations.

Important Notice

The concepts discussed on this site are controversial and often rejected by courts. Always consult a licensed professional before acting on anything related to law, taxes, debt, or court cases.

About the Teacher

Juan Dunlap is an independent researcher and educator who studies the intersection of law, history, finance, and personal sovereignty. He has spent years exploring how concepts like trusts, estates, and citizenship are interpreted both in mainstream law and in alternative “redemption” teachings.

Through classes, live sessions, and community discussions, Juan focuses on helping people slow down, ask better questions, and distinguish between historical facts, legal doctrine, and personal belief. He encourages every student to verify sources, consult qualified professionals, and make informed, responsible decisions for themselves.

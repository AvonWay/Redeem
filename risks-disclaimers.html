Risks, Warnings & Disclaimers

Understand the legal and practical risks of using “redemption” or “sovereign” strategies in real‑world situations.

Legal Risks

  • Courts have repeatedly rejected theories about strawmen, secret accounts, and unilateral “contracts” with government.
  • Filing false liens, pseudo‑financial instruments, or deceptive documents can lead to serious criminal charges.
  • Refusing to recognize a court’s jurisdiction based on these theories often leads to contempt findings and harsher outcomes.

Practical Risks

  • Following unverified templates from the internet in tax, foreclosure, child custody, or criminal cases can make your situation worse, not better.
  • Paying large sums for “gurus,” seminars, or document packages does not guarantee any legal result and may be a scam.
